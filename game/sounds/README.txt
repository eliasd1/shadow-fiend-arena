SHADOW FIEND ARENA - CUSTOM SOUNDS

The game has its own built-in sounds and a recorded announcer
("Announcer kill sounds" by TripleSnail, voice by Antti Saari, CC-BY 3.0). To replace any of them, put an audio
file in this folder (next to play.html) with one of the names below.
.ogg, .mp3 and .wav all work. Restart the game after adding files.

  raze          the raze eruption
  cast          the charge-up while a raze is being cast
  hit           a hero getting hit
  death         a hero dying
  soul          souls flying into you
  kill          you got a kill
  respawn       you respawn
  heartbeat     low-health heartbeat
  rune          picking up a rune
  ambient       background loop (plays on repeat)

Announcer:
  firstblood  doublekill  triplekill  massacre  rampage
  killingspree  dominating  megakill  mayhem  ownage  carnage  godlike

Example: sounds/raze.wav, sounds/firstblood.mp3

Only use sound files you have the right to use. For example, sounds you
extract from your own copy of Warcraft III are fine for playing at home,
but not for sharing or publishing the game.
